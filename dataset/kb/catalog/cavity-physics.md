### Resonances in the Earth-Ionosphere Cavity · `directional`
**E_r ∝ P_n(cos θ) and H_φ ∝ P_n^1(cos θ) in source–observer angle; a station's modal amplitude is source strength times a Legendre weight and is non-monotonic with range.**
- **Implication:** The cavity globe paints |P_n^1| patterns from the active chimney; a one-station amplitude is not a global storm-power meter.
- **Identifier:** `10.1007/978-94-017-0395-6`
- **Verify:** default unverified (existence may be gated; claim not abstract-NLI-supported)
- **Sources:** [Resonances in the Earth-Ionosphere Cavity](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-94-017-0395-6)
