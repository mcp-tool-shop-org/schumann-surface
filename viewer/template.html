Schumann Surface

What you are looking at

Time runs left to right across one month. Frequency runs front to back. Height and colour are magnetic amplitude in dB re 1 pT/√Hz.

The ridges running the full length are the Schumann resonances: the Earth–ionosphere cavity ringing near 7.8, 14, 20, 26 and 33 Hz. Lightning drives them, so they breathe once a day as the big thunderstorm regions (Asia, then Africa, then the Americas) rotate under the sun.
