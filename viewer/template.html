Modes

One-station inversion. Atlas: station at the centre, radius = angular distance, gold slices = measured polarisation (180° ambiguous). Globe is the same data on a sphere. Modes 4 and up are not in the data. Chimney discs are a model schedule, not a lightning map.

Time

Default 6×: one day is a short step against the frequency × amplitude plane, so the diurnal helix reads as a coil. 1× is the raw month-long streak.

Dose-response

Kp classes from Cann 2026: < 5, 5–6.7, ≥ 7. Month-demeaned subtracts that month's median. Bootstrap 1000, seed 13. The ≥ 7 class is two days; they are named, not smoothed.

What to look for

Rest state is the atlas: Sierra Nevada at the centre, rim = antipode. Gold pie slices are the measured bearing (two opposite lobes). Concentric red/green/blue rings are modes 1–3. Labelled discs are the three chimneys at their true range and bearing; disc size is the UTC model. Scrub to ~14 UTC: Africa should sit in a slice. ~20 UTC: the Americas. Globe is a toggle, not the default.

What to look for

Grey tube is the month-mean diurnal coil (amplitude from diurnal). Time is compressed so one day is a short step; use the slider if you want the raw streak. The coloured path is the data (Kp as colour). The yellow bead with the halo is the flagged storm. A path that leaves the coil is a departure, not a fourth mode.

What to look for

The 2013→2017 hole is a gap, not a join. Two storm days (1 Jun and 2 Oct 2013) are marked. The grey band is the quiet-class interval (the null). If ≥ 7 sits inside it after month-demeaning, this station does not show a season-controlled storm effect. n = 2 is two days.

Correlation, with the null shown

Pearson r at each lag, ±24 h. The grey band is the 95 % null with an effective sample size corrected for autocorrelation in both series (Bartlett). A curve that stays inside the band is a null result, and that is a valid result.
